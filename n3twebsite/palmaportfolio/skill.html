DESIGNS


My technical expertise in Graphic Design is highlighted by my recent work in Multimedia, where I transformed conceptual ideas into professional brand assets. I successfully managed three distinct design projects for student clients, each requiring a different visual strategy. This included creating a sleek, modern identity and logo for a local Vape Shop, as well as designing high-conversion marketing flyers for both a Coffee Shop and a Street Food business. These projects allowed me to master the balance between aesthetic appeal and functional communication, ensuring that every layout—from the minimalist elegance of a cafe to the vibrant energy of street food—spoke directly to the target audience. My ability to switch between these different design languages demonstrates my versatility and my commitment to delivering high-quality, client-focused visual solutions.

Visual Storytelling through Photography


Complementing my technical skills is a deep-seated passion for photography, where I capture the world through a lens of composition, lighting, and narrative. My experience in photography has sharpened my ability to spot the "perfect shot," a skill that translates directly into my web design work by helping me understand visual hierarchy and balance. Whether I am capturing portraits, landscapes, or urban scenes, I focus on how colors and framing evoke emotion and engagement. This artistic perspective allows me to produce and select high-quality imagery that doesn't just fill space on a website, but enhances the overall brand story and creates a more immersive digital experience.

Technical Versatility & Coding Proficiency


My foundation as a developer is built on a diverse toolkit of programming and scripting languages, allowing me to tackle both the logic and the layout of digital products. I am proficient in HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript, the core trio of the modern web, which I use to build interactive, responsive, and user-centric interfaces. Beyond the front-end, I have a strong grasp of Java and Python for structural programming, as well as SQL for managing and querying complex databases. This blend of creative styling, back-end logic, and data management means I don't just design websites that look good—I build robust, functional applications that are powered by efficient data structures.

Network Architecture & Connectivity


My technical expertise extends beyond software development into the vital realm of Networking. I have a solid understanding of how data moves across the globe, with specialized experience in designing, configuring, and troubleshooting network topologies using Cisco Packet Tracer. From setting up virtual routers and switches to implementing secure IP addressing schemes, I understand the underlying infrastructure that supports modern web applications. This networking knowledge allows me to approach web design with a "full-picture" mindset—ensuring that the sites I build aren't just aesthetically pleasing, but are optimized for the complex environments and protocols they live on.

Leadership

I translate this same military-grade discipline into my web design projects—ensuring that every team I lead stays organized, every deadline is met with precision, and every project reflects a high standard of excellence.

Creativity
